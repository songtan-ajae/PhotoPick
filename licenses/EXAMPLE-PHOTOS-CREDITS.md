## Synthetic & Educational Disclosure
These images are provided solely as offline explanatory examples ("설명용 예시"). Their display does not imply any cloud ranking, personal preference judgment, or artistic evaluation of the user's personal photos.

## Widget Rendering & Illustrative Display Transformations
In addition to the unmodified licensed photographic assets, PhotoPick UI widgets may render local display-only illustrative visual transformations (such as widget-level ImageFilter blur, brightness dimming, or contrast reduction) alongside the original image. These side-by-side treatments serve solely to help users visually distinguish individual metric dimensions (e.g. sharp contour vs. blurred contour, balanced vs. darkened exposure, rich vs. muted contrast) without modifying the underlying asset files and without making subjective "better/worse" quality verdicts.
